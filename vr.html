🥽 The VR Parlor

Put on your headset browser (Quest Browser or Vision Pro Safari), then press Enter VR. Each eye gets its own half of the card, exactly like a Victorian stereoscope, so the depth just works. Each card auto aligns itself and tucks its depth behind the frame as it loads. Trigger or tap a plaque to flip cards, point at the thumbnails below to jump, and on a flat screen just drag to look around.
