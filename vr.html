🥽 The VR Parlor

Put on your headset browser (Quest Browser or Vision Pro Safari), then press Enter VR. Each eye gets its own half of the card, exactly like a Victorian stereoscope, so the depth just works. Every view is aligned by a measuring pipeline before it hangs here, so your eyes can relax. There is no frame and no wall: the photograph hangs far away in the dark, enlarged, its edges feathered into the black. Trigger flips forward, squeeze goes back.
