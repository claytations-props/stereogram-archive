🥽 The VR Parlor

Put on your headset browser (Quest Browser or Vision Pro Safari), then press Enter VR. Each eye gets its own half of the card, exactly like a Victorian stereoscope, so the depth just works. Every view is cropped to the printed photograph itself and run through a measuring pipeline before it hangs here: the two halves are matched feature by feature, rotated and scaled into register, brightness matched, and the depth checked, and deep views are hung a little smaller so they stay comfortable. There is no frame and no wall: the photograph hangs far away in the dark, enlarged, its edges feathered into the black, so you look straight into the scene itself. Trigger or tap a plaque to flip cards, point at the thumbnails below to jump, and on a flat screen just drag to look around.
