The Stereograph Archive

🥽 The VR Parlor

Put on your headset browser (Quest Browser or Vision Pro Safari), then press Enter VR. Each eye gets its own half of the card, exactly like a Victorian stereoscope, so the depth just works. Every view is aligned by a measuring pipeline before it hangs here, so your eyes can relax. There is no frame and no wall: the photograph hangs far away in the dark, enlarged, its edges feathered into the black. Trigger flips forward, squeeze goes back, and you can also point at the row of buttons beneath the caption or flick the thumbstick. The double arrows jump ten cards at a time, and the EXIT button in that row takes you out of VR whenever you are ready. This room is the archive itself: the Browse button opens the finding aid and a wall of the current shelf right here, so you can pick a collection or series and point at any card to open it, and the Record button shows the item's catalogue card.
