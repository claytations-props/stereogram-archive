The VR Parlor

Use a headset browser (Quest Browser or Vision Pro Safari), then press Enter VR. Each eye gets its own half of the card, exactly like a Victorian stereoscope, and every view is aligned before it hangs here, so your eyes can relax. Trigger flips forward, squeeze goes back; the button row beneath the caption does the same, and its double arrows jump ten cards. Browse opens the shelves inside the room, Record shows the catalogue card, and EXIT leaves VR.
