How to see the 3D

Pair: the card as it was sold. In a real stereoscope each eye got one half.  Parallel: relax your eyes like you are looking through the screen until the two halves merge into a third image in the middle.  Cross-eye: gently cross your eyes until the halves overlap, best for some people and works big on a screen.  Wiggle: no eye tricks needed, the card flickers between left and right and your brain reads the depth anyway.  Anaglyph: wear red/cyan glasses, red on the left eye. Alignment is approximate because these old cards were never perfectly trimmed.
